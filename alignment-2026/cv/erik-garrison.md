## Research expertise and role

We develop methods that reveal relationships among complete genome assemblies without privileging one linear reference. Garrison led the development of `vg` and develops `impg`, alignment, and pangenome methods used by the Human Pangenome Reference Consortium. For this project, he directs the scientific design, alignment methodology, implicit-pangenome analysis, performance evaluation, and public release of the vertebrate alignment atlas.
